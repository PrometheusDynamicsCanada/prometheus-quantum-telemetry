Mechanistic operating-envelope experiments (01-05) investigating how physical routing overhead, regional coupler error heterogeneity, and target-QPU selection interact on 156-qubit IBM Heron processors.

**Dataset Scope:** 14 IBM Quantum jobs (774 physical PUBs, 4,125,440 physical shots across all 14 archived `job-*-result.json` measurement arrays; 417 normalized circuit rows and 1,671,168 shots in [`MASTER_RESULTS.csv`](MASTER_RESULTS.csv) for Experiments 01-03), 73 logical OpenQASM circuits, SHA-256 integrity manifests ([`IBM_JOB_EVIDENCE_INDEX.csv`](IBM_JOB_EVIDENCE_INDEX.csv)), and standalone offline verifiers.

## Measuring Where Calibration-Aware Routing Helps - and Where Gate Overhead Hurts

### The Customer Problem
Calibration-aware quantum routing faces an empirical trade-off: detouring around higher-error couplers or qubits can improve gate quality, but routing detours also add two-qubit operations (`3 CZ` per `SWAP` on IBM Heron). On dense circuits, wider subgraphs, or more uniformly calibrated chip regions, extra two-qubit gate overhead can outweigh any benefit from avoiding noisy couplers.

### What Prometheus Does
**Prometheus evaluates calibration-aware routing alongside Qiskit SABRE O3 and Quantinuum TKET across 156-qubit IBM Heron processors (`ibm_fez`, `ibm_marrakesh`, `ibm_kingston`), and this repository documents both positive regimes and measured negative boundaries across 14 jobs, 774 PUBs, and 4,125,440 physical shots.**

### Three Key Empirical Results Across Tested Workloads and Processors
1. **30/40 (75.0%) Fidelity Wins Alongside Measured Gate-Cost Trade-Offs (`Experiment 02`, `Section 1`):** Across 40 matched randomized QAOA and Cross-Entanglement pairs (`6Q-9Q`, 120 PUBs in `job-da1httsdedkc73er2k70` on `ibm_marrakesh`), calibration-aware routing achieved higher Squared Hellinger Fidelity F_H than Qiskit SABRE O3 in **30/40 (75.0%)** instances while losing in **10/40 (25.0%)** instances.
   *Supporting metrics:* Recomputed from `logical_abstract_2q_gates + routing_induced_2q_overhead` (`02-randomized-instance-robustness/scripts/analyze_matched_pairs.py`), all 40 instances used higher routed two-qubit gate counts than SABRE O3 (`+199, +348, +644, +971` mean gates on `6Q-9Q` Cross-Entanglement), winning 4/5 on `9Q` CrossEnt (e.g., 0.7450 vs. 0.6438) while trailing in 10/40 pairs where extra gate cost was not recovered.
2. **Processor- and Width-Dependent Outcomes Across `ibm_marrakesh`, `ibm_fez`, and `ibm_kingston` (`Experiment 05`, `Section 1`):** Across 540 physical circuits (180 PUBs × 3 QPUs × 2,048 shots = 1,105,920 shots), calibration-aware routing won **67%** of conditional `N in {6, 7}` QAOA comparisons on **`ibm_marrakesh`**, while underperforming SABRE O3 on the tested sectors of `ibm_fez` and `ibm_kingston` and on `N = 8` instances.
   *Supporting metrics:* Demonstrates empirically that detour-routing gains observed on one processor and width cohort do not automatically transfer to more uniform calibration sectors or denser widths.
3. **Observed `6Q-9Q` vs. `10Q` Crossover and Distributional Diagnostics (`Experiments 01, 03 & 04`, `Section 1`):** On tested QFT and QAOA instances (`Experiment 03`, `3Q-10Q`, 96 PUBs, 960,000 shots on `ibm_marrakesh`), positive `F_H` deltas at `6Q-9Q` (`QFT-9 +0.1197`, `QAOA-6 +0.1627`, `QAOA-9 +0.1461`) reversed at `10Q` (`QAOA-10 -0.0591` with +89 2Q gates; `QFT-10 -0.0306` with +432 2Q gates).
   *Supporting metrics:* `Experiment 01` (8 jobs, up to 100,000 shots/circuit) showed output entropy (`H = 6.6045` vs. 7.9554 bits) and `D_KL` against the ideal distribution can move independently, and `Experiment 04` (`13Q` CrossEnt, 80,000 shots/PUB) reduced all-zero bitstring pooling by **-62.9%** (206 vs. 555 shots).

## 1. Executive Summary of Mechanistic Experiments (01 - 05)

> [!NOTE]
> **Metric & Direction Conventions:**
> - **Squared Hellinger Fidelity (F_H = F_B², ↑ higher is better):** In this repository (`hellinger_fidelity` in [`MASTER_RESULTS.csv`](MASTER_RESULTS.csv) and 02/03 `summary.csv`), fidelity is defined as F_H(P_ideal, P_meas) = (Σ_x √(P_ideal(x) · P_meas(x)))² = F_B² (where F_B = √(F_H) is the unsquared Bhattacharyya overlap). Both evaluate computational-basis measurement distributions from shot histograms and are distinct from 4ⁿ state tomography.
> - **Total Variation Distance (TVD, ↓ lower is better):** 0.5 · Σ_x |P_ideal(x) - P_meas(x)|.
> - **Shannon Entropy (H, context-dependent):** -Σ_x P_meas(x) log₂ P_meas(x) in bits (measures distributional concentration/spread rather than agreement with P_ideal).

## 3. Scope & Interpretation Boundaries

The experiments in this repository do not claim universal compiler superiority, guaranteed fidelity improvement from adding routing operations, or a single physical variable that determines execution quality. Negative and mixed outcomes (such as the 10/40 routing-penalty cases in Experiment 02, the 10Q crossover in Experiment 03, and the homogeneous-QPU results in Experiment 05) are preserved in full because they define the empirical operating boundary of hardware-aware routing.
